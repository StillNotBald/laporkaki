## 2. Who fixes what

- Roads: federal roads → JKR (KKR); state roads → JKR for the state; local/residential roads → council; expressways → LLM/concessionaire. [stated] https://policystreet.com.my/en/blog/pothole-damage-claim-guide-malaysia ; https://www.motorist.my/article/6488/pothole-damage-to-your-car-in-malaysia ; road-code heuristic (FT routes federal, letter-coded state, un-coded local) is a forum claim [stated, forum] https://www.reddit.com/r/malaysia/comments/1g39ts0/
- Streetlights: TNB-owned lamps → TNB Careline/SMS 15454 or myTNB app; council-owned lamps → council; myTNB distinguishes "TNB-owned vs third-party" lights. [stated] https://www.mytnb.com.my/business/street-lighting ; https://www.tnb.com.my/contact-us/customer-care . Concrete pole = TNB, steel pole = council is a forum rule of thumb [stated, forum] https://www.reddit.com/r/malaysia/comments/1c5z3ud/ . Highway lights → concessionaire [inferred].
- Drains: roadside/monsoon drains and broken covers → council [stated, SAYS]; major road drains on federal/state roads → JKR [stated, SAYS]; rivers and flood mitigation → JPS (DID) [inferred — standard jurisdiction, no page fetched].
- Water pipes/leaks/no water: Air Selangor (KL + Selangor); app/web "Report an issue" (pipe burst/leak/theft), careline 15300. [stated] https://www.airselangor.com/support/report ; https://www.airselangor.com/whats-happening/campaigns/reportleakscampaign
- Sewerage (manhole overflow, smell): Indah Water Konsortium, care@iwk.com.my, 03-2083 2828. [stated] https://www.iwk.com.my/contact-us
- Rubbish collection: KL/Putrajaya → Alam Flora under SWCorp regulation (Alam Flora 1-800-88-7472, WhatsApp 019-231-7819; SWCorp 03-8312 4000). [stated] https://www.swcorp.gov.my/en/frequently-asked-questions-faq/ ; https://alamflora.com.my/index.php/contact/ . Selangor → KDEB Waste Management (i-Clean Selangor app; 1-800-88-2824, WhatsApp 019-274 2824). [stated] https://www.kdebwm.com/media-room/articles/kdebwm-36-800-waste-disposal-complaints-via-i-clea/
- Illegal dumping: cleanup KDEB (Selangor)/Alam Flora (KL); enforcement SWCorp/council [inferred from SWCorp's regulator role, FAQ above].
- Fallen trees: tree on federal road → JKR (court held JKR liable for an uprooted tree injuring a motorcyclist) [stated] https://theedgemalaysia.com/node/668572 ; council-road/park trees → council [inferred]; report via MyJalan (road obstruction category) [stated] https://www.malaysia.gov.my/en/categories/safety-and-community/road-safety/myjalan-kkr-application
- Abandoned vehicles: council enforcement/towing [inferred]; scale: 60,000+ reported nationally 2018–2023, KL 9,000+ [stated] https://malaysia.news.yahoo.com/abandoned-vehicles-indicator-deeper-social-012034884.html
- Traffic lights: JKR on federal/state roads, reportable via MyJalan [stated, malaysia.gov.my above]; DBKL signals in KL → DBKL [inferred].
- Broken playground, faded road paint: playground → council parks dept [inferred]; road markings → whoever owns the road (JKR/council/concessionaire) [inferred].
- Flooding: flash flood from blocked drains → council; river overflow → JPS; disaster response → NADMA/APM [inferred].

## 3. National and state channels

- SISPAA (Sistem Pengurusan Aduan Awam), run by BPA/PCB under the PM's Dept: one complaint platform, each agency gets its own subdomain (pcb.spab.gov.my, selangor.spab.gov.my, mbsa.spab.gov.my, ppj.spab.gov.my…). Submit, get case ID, track status. [stated] https://pcb.spab.gov.my/eApps/system/index.do?aplCode=en ; https://www.malaysia.gov.my/feedback/integrated-complaint-management-system-sispaa
- Selangor state portal: https://selangor.spab.gov.my (#KitaSelangor) [stated]. Councils also sit on SISPAA (e.g. MBSA) [stated, FB post §1].
- Pain points: registration/account needed before filing [stated, SAYS]; wrong-agency reports cause delays [stated, SAYS]; a 2025/26 UTM paper had to build a dashboard because SISPAA lacks usable monitoring/visualisation for handlers [stated] https://oiji.utm.my/index.php/oiji/article/download/340/246/1390 ; ~2/3 of MyJalan road complaints were NOT KKR roads — citizens cannot tell jurisdictions apart [stated] https://international.astroawani.com/malaysia-berita/beyond-pothole-when-simple-complaint-became-new-way-looking-malaysias-roads
- Public resolution stats: some agencies publish them — Perbadanan Putrajaya: all 1,632 SISPAA complaints of 2025 resolved by 31 Dec 2025 [stated] https://smart.putrajaya.my/en/initiatives/e-aduan/ . No unified public per-council resolution league table: not found.

## 4. Existing apps (one line each)

- MyJalan (KKR): photo + location road-defect reports, "No Wrong Door" auto-routing to JKR/council/state; 24h pothole-patch SOP for federal roads; >54,000 complaints by Feb 2026, KKR claims ~95.8% resolved (own roads). Roads only; no AI triage of arbitrary problems. [stated] https://www.malaysia.gov.my/en/categories/safety-and-community/road-safety/myjalan-kkr-application ; https://www.thestar.com.my/news/nation/2025/08/06/authorities-must-fix-potholes-reported-via-myjalan-app-in-24-hours-says-ahmad-maslan ; https://www.nst.com.my/news/nation/2026/02/1383720/over-54000-road-damage-complaints-logged-through-myjalan ; https://www.bernama.com/en/news.php?id=2499986
- Respons Rakyat V2 (PCB): snap photo, pin location, track; front-end to government complaints — no fix suggestions, no public accountability layer. [stated] https://www.pcb.gov.my/my/info-aduan/respons-rakyat (via SAYS)
- myDBKL: KL-only, manual category picker (drains/roads/lights); user must already know the category. [stated, SAYS]
- i-Clean Selangor (KDEB): waste complaints only, Selangor only; 36,800 resolved in 2022. [stated] https://www.kdebwm.com/media-room/articles/kdebwm-36-800-waste-disposal-complaints-via-i-clea/
- myTNB: streetlight/power faults only. [stated] https://www.mytnb.com.my/business/street-lighting
- Waze in-app reports: crowdsourced hazards visible to drivers, but reports never become tickets to any fixing body. [inferred — common knowledge, no page fetched]
- FixMyStreet-style independent Malaysian civic platform with per-council routing: not found. Gap: one photo → named problem → correct body → tracked fix, across all problem types.

## 5. Volunteer / donation angle

- The Brotherhood (founded 2007 by actor Lando Zawawi): volunteers patch potholes nationwide with own funds, claim thousands fixed. [stated] https://vulcanpost.com/730256/the-brotherhood-malaysians-fixing-potholes-road-safety/
- Legal limit: in 2014 KL DIY pothole fixers were told by authorities to stop — unauthorised work on public roads is not permitted. [stated] https://www.bbc.com/news/blogs-news-from-elsewhere-30377621 . So the product should route + fund authorised fixes (council-approved contractors, CSR), never encourage DIY road works [inferred].
- Fundraising rules (one paragraph): public collections in Malaysia fall under the House to House and Street Collections Act 1947 (Act 200) — house-to-house and street collections need a police permit, and approvals from the relevant state authority may also apply [stated] https://alim.my/legislation/act-200/ ; https://themalaysianreserve.com/2024/12/18/specific-law-needed-to-monitor-regulate-online-donations/ . Online donations sit in a legal grey zone — commentators note Act 200 predates the internet and call for a specific law [stated, same two URLs; also https://donorcare.my/resources/fundraising-rules-malaysia ]. Safest demo posture: partner with an existing permitted body rather than collect money in-app [inferred].
