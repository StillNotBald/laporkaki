# 05 — Pilot apps: who else is building this, the strata-app pattern, and where to pilot

Researched 2026-11-19 via web search/fetch. Builds on `research/03-routing.md` ("03 §N") and `research/04-landscape.md` ("04 §N"); not re-fetched. `[stated]` = source says it (URL inline); `[inferred]` = our judgement. "not found" = searched, nothing published located. Statistics carry as-of dates. Demo uses a simulated council inbox — no real partnership exists or is implied.

**TL;DR:** Malaysia already has working report-to-fix loops, but each is fenced in — councils run SISPAA/e-Aduan silos (DBKL's new Adu@KL claims 70k+ resolved but went offline for weeks in 2025), utilities run single-issue apps (i-Clean: 150k users, ~98% within SLA), and strata apps (JagaApp 500+ neighbourhoods, iNeighbour 650+ communities) prove residents happily use report→management-inbox→status→resolved inside a gate. Nobody runs that loop for public space across agencies; the closest indie effort (JomLapor) is single-issue NGO advocacy, and Putrajaya's own smart-city blueprint explicitly plans "AI complaint categorisation" — the government itself has named our core feature as the next step. Best pilot: Petaling Jaya (MBPJ) — documented resident organising, the valley's most mature multi-channel council, and mixed problem types that show off routing. ### The pattern and the gap
Pattern (proven at 500–650+ communities each): resident reports (photo/QR/text) → **management (JMB/MC) inbox that must respond** (they collect the maintenance fees) → status updates → resolved, all inside a defined boundary. Two-way feedback, noticeboard push, and management analytics are standard. [stated features above; synthesis inferred]
Gap, precisely: **these loops stop at the strata/gated boundary.** The pothole on the public road outside the gate, the council drain, the TNB streetlight, the illegal dumping on public land have no equivalent inbox-with-a-duty-to-respond — SISPAA exists but is registration-walled, agency-siloed and publicly opaque (03 §3, 04 §3). LaporKaki is the JagaApp-style loop for public space: one photo → AI triage → the right body's inbox → public status → fix clock. [inferred]
Borrowable for the demo: ticket statuses + two-way reply thread (JagaApp Feedback), resident push notifications (Noticeboard), **QR-on-asset reporting** (iNeighbour Maintenance — matches our QR-sticker idea, 04 §5), management dashboard with time-spent/backlog analytics (iNeighbour Analytics), "me too" (FMS Pro, 04 §2). Pricing precedent: the responsible body pays a monthly subscription; citizens free — mirrors SeeClickFix (04 §5). [stated features; mapping inferred]
